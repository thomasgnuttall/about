I am an AI researcher, engineer, and musician who most recently completed my PhD in the Music Technology Group (MTG) at the Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF), Barcelona, where I led the Indian Art Music branch of the CompMusic project - a cross-disciplinary project that connects musicians and musicologists with engineers to develop computational tools for musicological research and performance.

Prior to academia, I worked on and led machine learning product development at Channel 4 Television, Glovo, Typeform, and Crowdlinker, focussing on user-facing personalization and recommendation products.

I form part of two Barcelona-based groups: (1) Mukti, a duo of myself and Jyoti Narang, combining North Indian folk singing with European dance/guitar traditions and (2) Awa Pa Los Calvos, combining flamenco with funk and electronica.
